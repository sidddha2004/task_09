Our Story

165 Years of Excellence

Founded in 1860 in the heart of Jodhpur, AdiShila has been a beacon of leather craftsmanship for over a century and a half. What began as a small family workshop has grown into a renowned name synonymous with quality, tradition, and innovation.

Every AdiShila product carries the legacy of generations of master craftsmen who have dedicated their lives to the art of leather work. Using techniques passed down through families, combined with modern design sensibilities, we create pieces that are built to last a lifetime.
